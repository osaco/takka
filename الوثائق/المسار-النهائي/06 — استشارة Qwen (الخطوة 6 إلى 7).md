```text
You are our Lead Chief Architect and Principal Game/Software Engineer with 30+ years of
experience shipping applications and games. Reviewing an offline-first educational mastery
engine for Grade-1 children in Gaza, wrapped in a single-island builder world.

CONSTITUTION: one deterministic 12x12 island; no timers, energy, gacha, ads, IAP, PvP,
leaderboards; zero runtime network; zero child data; 9 buildings revealed 3/3/3 by HQ level;
Canvas draws shapes, every Arabic string stays in DOM; touch >= 56dp; reduced motion fully
respected; test count rises monotonically.

VERIFIED: 144/144 tests across 16 files, typecheck 0 errors, build 2.01s.
Bundle: app 326.16 KB raw / 97.13 gzip. Economy proven (143 stars / 14 materials spend vs
170 / 17 income; 18.9% and 21.4% surplus).

STEP 6 (just completed): read-only binding of the stage to island state.
- `buildStageView(hqLevel, placement)` is the single read model: one source for reveal state
  and position, so they cannot disagree.
- Locked buildings are not hidden: 40% opacity, no path dot, no halo, plus a drawn padlock
  (shape, not colour — survives colour blindness). Long-press shows the Arabic hint.
- Long-press: 500ms timer, cancelled by >10px movement, so Step 7 drag will not fight it.
  Locked tiles start no timer and ignore taps.
- Incomplete placement merges missing buildings from the default layout; corrupt placement
  replaces the whole state. A building is never silently dropped from the island.
- Store now carries `island` and `placement`, loaded from Dexie at boot inside try/catch.

STEP 7 (next): make the HQ tower live.
- Show current level and upgrade cost from the upgrade table.
- Confirm payment, deduct resources, upgrade instantly (no build timer) with a <=800ms
  decorative effect that never blocks input.
- Warm, non-blaming message when resources are short.
- 8 tests: happy path, rejection on shortfall, and newly revealed buildings after upgrade.
- Exit criterion 152/152.

THE OBSTACLE: `EconomyState` does not exist in the runtime store yet, and nothing grants
stars when a skill is mastered. Without it, the upgrade button would deduct nothing.

QUESTIONS — answer each explicitly:

1. ECONOMY WIRING: Where should the first-mastery grant fire — inside the mastery commit
   action (after evidence is persisted), or in a separate effect reacting to mastery change?
   The risk is a double grant on retry/replay. Give the exact call site and the guard.

2. PERSISTENCE: EconomyState must survive battery loss. Do we store it as a row in Dexie
   (new table), or derive it from the evidence log on boot? Derivation is self-healing but
   O(n) and cannot represent spent resources. Pick one and justify for a 2GB device.

3. UPGRADE TRANSACTION: The upgrade touches economy, island state, and placement. Should it
   be one Dexie transaction, or sequential pure updates with a single save? What is the
   failure mode you would test first?

4. THE 800ms EFFECT: what exactly should animate so a 7-year-old reads "the tower grew",
   without motion sickness and without blocking input, and what replaces it under
   prefers-reduced-motion?

5. SHORTFALL COPY: give me three Arabic microcopy lines for insufficient resources that a
   child hears as encouragement, not failure. No blame, no "you cannot".

SCORING out of 10 with the single most important fix below 9:
A. Read-model design and lock affordance.
B. Gesture state machine and Step 7 readiness.
C. Test design.
D. Execution risk of the remaining 4 steps.

Close with a numbered checklist for Step 7 I can execute without further clarification.
```
