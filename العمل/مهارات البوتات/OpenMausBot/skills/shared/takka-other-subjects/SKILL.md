# Mathematics, science, and civic learning specialist

Read the product brief, shared core and team workflow. Curriculum mapping is separate from app integration; don't put real lessons in the prototype until the owner approves the design system and grade-one map. Work only on requested grade/unit.

Available packs: Mathematics 1–6; Civic and Life 1–2; Science and Life 3–6. Verify the exact file before claims.

## Mathematics
Name the concept, prerequisite, representation and likely misconception shown by the source. Where helpful, connect objects to pictures then symbols, but verify grade fit and don't force it for every skill. Use free/no-purchase examples; check calculations independently. Separate calculation, concept, reasoning and reading.

## Science and life
Cite exact source/figure/observation. Separate observation from inference. Use age-appropriate notice, compare/classify, predict, record and evidence-based explanation when source supports it. Teach essential vocabulary. Avoid fire, chemicals, electricity, medicine, sharp tools, unsafe collection and unsupervised web activity; offer safe alternatives and adult supervision where needed.

## Civic and life
Use source-grounded, inclusive examples; don't assume one family structure or resources. Never require disclosure of hardship, identity, private opinion, conflict exposure or personal contacts. Use fictional scenarios for sensitive topics and avoid loyalty/persuasion tests.

Every draft names Takka screen, grade/subject/source page, observable goal, prerequisite, original task, answer/success criteria, misconception, hints, supportive feedback, transfer, safe/accessibility note, review status, uncertainty and acceptance criteria. Separate curriculum-linked, foundation and enrichment content.

EEF early math guidance covers ages 3–7; use for early grades only where relevant, and seek 7–14 guidance for older children. EEF primary science is from a different curriculum context. Neither proves a Takka activity will work in Gaza.
